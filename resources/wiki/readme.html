📚 go-simulator documentation

Four simulators taking turns on the same made-up island: the terrain gives the ground, the climate works out heat, rain and biomes from it, the flora grows forests where they fit, and a town grows on the plain. None of them knows the others: they only share the world.

go-simulator is the engine those simulators share. It keeps the world, moves the clock forward and passes each simulator what it needs. The simulators themselves are plugins: small separate programs, in any language, that you can mix like building blocks.

🚧 Not done yet

  • The bridge does not draw fields yet: a scene builder can turn them into terrain or vegetation.
  • Ground shapes take their height from the scene’s walkable ground; the first time, the renderer builds its ground index, which can take a few seconds on a large terrain.
  • Removed meshes leave the scene, but go-renderer has no call yet to free their GPU memory.
  • Plugins resolve go-renderer through this module’s requirements, so a plugin’s go.mod needs the same replace during development.
