🏷️ Labels, header and notices

Besides the scene, the live viewer (cmd/simulator) writes a few words over it: what a boat carries, what time it is in the simulation, what just happened. Plugins do not draw any of it. They set a few overlay properties on their entities and a notice on their events, and the bridge turns them into labels, a header and notices.

The pictures on this page are captures of the viewer (-capture), by day and with -night, of Go Renderer’s made-up sample island and a small made-up plugin that sails three boats round it.

Labels

An entity with a label, an icon or a badge gets a light pill above it, with the badge in a smaller pill underneath. Labels keep their size on screen at any zoom, follow their entity as it moves and disappear when it is off screen, hidden or removed. When two would overlap, the one nearer the camera is shown.

{"op": "add", "entity": {"id": "ferry", "kind": "boat", "shape": {"primitive": "box", "size": [4, 2, 10]},
  "properties": {"label": "Ferry → quay", "icon": "ship", "badge": "12/40", "label_color": "#2563eb"}}}
Property Value Shown as
label text a short text in a light pill above the entity
icon text: a Lucide icon name an icon before the label, or alone in a circle
badge text (or a number) a very short text in a small pill under the label: 7/10
label_color "#rrggbb" the colour of the icon and the badge; the text stays dark so it always reads well

How high a label floats:

Shape Label anchored at
box the top of the box
a mesh of the scene its highest point
outline over the middle of the outline, at its height (just over the ground when flat)
line over its middle point, at its height
none the entity’s position

The header and notices

  • The header: entities with pinned (a number) are listed in a card at the top-left corner, by that number (1 first) instead of above the entity; a badge follows the text after a dot. Such entities need no shape. A clock and a few counters fit well there.
  • Notices: events with a notice appear under the header, newest first, for about six seconds of the viewer’s time, then fade. An event can add an icon and a label_color.
{"op": "add", "entity": {"id": "harbour-clock", "properties": {"label": "08:25", "icon": "clock", "pinned": 1}}}
{"op": "emit", "event": {"name": "arrivals", "data": {"notice": "Boat 4 moored at the north quay", "icon": "anchor"}}}

On the map

The overlay is drawn in every view of the viewer: orbiting, walking, and the map (-map, or M).

Good to know

  • Property-only changes (a modify with only properties) update the label at once; setting a property to null takes it away.
  • Captures (-capture) include the overlay.
  • simulate has no window and ignores the overlay, but its snapshots keep the properties.
  • Models should not read the overlay properties: they are for whoever watches the run.
